# Your website: a simple guide

You can change almost everything on your website yourself, from your phone or computer, in a couple of minutes. **You never need to touch code, and you never need to ask your developer for photo, video, text or link changes.** When you save, your website is updated the next time anyone opens it.

## What's already on your site

Your website already has everything on it, the same as before: sample photos, a portrait, a short bio, reviews, FAQ answers and the hero video. **All of it is placeholder content for you to swap for your own.** Nothing has been taken away.

Open the **Dashboard**. Under **Needs your attention** it lists what's still a placeholder (for example "13 portfolio items, 3 reviews, 2 FAQ answers, your biography, your portrait"), with a **Start replacing** button. Anything still a placeholder has a **Placeholder: replace me** badge, and the badge disappears by itself the moment you replace it. You can work through them in any order, a few at a time.

- **Photos:** *Work → Manage Work* → tap a photo → **Replace** the picture, change the title and description. Or delete the sample and **Add** your own.
- **Portrait and bio:** *Media → Profile Photo* and *Website Content → About*.
- **Reviews:** *Reviews*: edit the `[Placeholder review]` text into a real one (with the client's OK), or delete it. Reviews that begin with `[Placeholder review]` are examples only.
- **FAQ:** *Website Content → FAQ*: two answers say `[Owner to review…]`; write your real answers.
- **Hero video:** *Hero*.

## Signing in

1. Open **p00rmans.github.io/hyrumcuts/admin** (or **yourdomain.com/admin** once a custom domain is connected).
2. Type your email and password, then tap **Sign in**.

**Put it on your phone's home screen** so it opens like an app, with its own "HH" icon:
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

**Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page tap **Forgot your password?**, type your email and tap **Email me a reset link**. Open the email (check spam too), tap the link, and choose a new password. Passwords need at least 12 characters, with a capital letter, a small letter and a number. A sentence works well: `Fresh-Fades-Every-Day-2026`.

To change your password later, or sign out on every device (for example if you lose your phone), go to **Password & Security**.

**Always sign out on a shared or borrowed computer.**

## Getting around

On your phone, use the bar along the bottom: **Home**, **Work**, **Add** (+), **Hero**, and **More** for everything else. On a computer the same menu is on the left.

## Adding photos to your portfolio

1. Tap **Add** (+) → **Photos**.
2. Tap **Choose photos**. On your phone you can pick from your photo library, or take a photo. You can pick **several at once**.
3. Each photo gets a small card. Fill in:
   - **Title**: for example, "Mid skin fade with textured top".
   - **Category**: Fades, Tapers, and so on.
   - **Alt text**: one short sentence saying what the photo shows, for people who can't see it (and for Google). Example: *"Side view of a mid skin fade blending into a textured crop."* You need this before you can publish. Write it yourself: automatic descriptions often get haircuts wrong.
4. Choose what happens:
   - **Publish now** ticked: it goes live on your website straight away.
   - **Publish now** not ticked: it's saved as a **draft** that only you can see.
5. Tap **Save all**. You'll see each photo turn to **Saved ✓**.

If one photo fails (bad connection, wrong file type) the others are still saved. The failed one stays on the screen so you can try again, and nothing half-saved is left behind.

**Before & after:** *Add → Before & after* lets you save one "before" and one "after" photo together.
**A video clip:** *Add → Video clip*. A poster picture is picked from the video automatically.

### Best photos

- Real photos of your own work, with the client's OK if their face is clearly visible.
- Any size is fine: the site shrinks them. Photos from a normal phone camera work great.
- **JPEG, PNG or WebP.** iPhone photos in "HEIC" format can't be used. Fix it once: **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible**.
- Location information is removed from every photo automatically.

## Publish, draft, hide, archive, delete

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Visible on your website. |
| **Draft** | Saved but hidden. Only you see it. Good for work you haven't finished describing. |
| **Hide** | Turns a published item back into a draft. |
| **Archive** | Hidden, but kept safely so you can **Restore** it later. |
| **Delete** | Erases it for good. You're asked to confirm first, and it can't be undone. |

Go to **Work → Manage Work** to see everything. There you can **search** by title, haircut or category, filter by category or status, **Preview** how a piece looks, **Feature** your best work (★), and change the order. Your search and filters stay put if you refresh the page, and the numbers on the **Dashboard** (Published, Drafts) open this list already filtered.

### Changing the order

On **Manage Work**, **drag** the ⋮⋮ handle up or down, **or** use the **↑ ↓** buttons. The order you set is the order on your website.

### Categories

**Work → Categories** lets you add, rename, reorder, hide and delete the filters above your gallery. A category only shows on your website when it has published work in it. If you delete a category that still has work in it, you're asked where to move that work first.

## Your hero video (the video at the very top)

**Hero** on the bottom bar (or *Media → Hero Video*).

What works best:

- Wide (landscape) video, **10–20 seconds**
- **MP4** (or WebM), no sound needed
- Around **1080p** and **under 20 MB**

Choose your video. A **poster picture** (the still image people see before it plays, or if they'd rather not have motion) is picked from the video automatically. Check it, or choose your own, then tap **Save hero media**. Big videos upload in pieces with a progress bar. If your connection drops, just tap **Save** again and it carries on. Your old hero stays in place until the new one has fully uploaded, so the site is never left without one.

If you shot on an iPhone and the file ends in **.MOV**, use *Photos → Share → Options → Most Compatible* (or export as MP4) first. If it's too big, trim it to 10–20 seconds or run it through a free compressor such as HandBrake.

## Sharing one cut

When you (or a client) open a photo in the gallery, the web address changes to point at that exact cut. Copy it from the address bar and send it: whoever opens the link lands straight on that photo, with the arrows to browse the rest. The viewer also shows where you are (for example **3 / 13**).

## Your portrait, bio and text

- **Media → Profile Photo**: the portrait beside your bio. Add a short description of the photo too.
- **Website Content → About**: your name, biography, specialties, general location and availability. Anything you leave empty is simply hidden. (Your About section appears once you add a biography or a photo.)
- **Website Content → Hero**: the words at the top of the home page and the labels on your Book buttons.
- **Website Content → FAQ**: questions people ask. Turn each one on or off, drag to reorder.
- **Website Content → Announcement**: a short notice across the top of every page, for example *"Away Oct 3–10. Booking reopens Oct 11."* You can add a link (like your booking page) and pick **Show from** / **Show until** dates, so it appears and disappears by itself. Dates follow your time zone (Settings → SEO). Clear the message to turn it off. Visitors can close it with the **×**.

## Appointment requests

When someone fills in the request form on your booking page, it lands in **Requests** (in the menu, and in the bottom bar on your phone). A small number next to **Requests** shows how many are new, and the **Dashboard** tells you too.

Each request shows the name, the day and time they'd like, the service, and any notes, with **Call**, **Text** and **Email** buttons that open your phone's apps. Then move it along with one tap: **Mark contacted**, **Mark booked**, or **Close** when you're done. Use the **Show** menu to see closed ones again.

Requests contain your clients' personal details and only you can see them. **Delete** a request when a client asks you to, or once you no longer need it.

## Reviews

**Reviews → + Add review.** Only add **real** reviews from clients who said you may share them. Use just a first name or initial. New reviews you add stay **hidden until you turn on "Approved and shown"**. Never write or change a review to say something the client didn't say.

## Booking, contact and social links

Under **Settings**:

- **Booking Link**: where the **Book** buttons go. Choose your provider, then paste its link. Choose **None** to use only the on-site request form.
  - **Google Calendar (free):** in Google Calendar, tap **Create → Appointment schedule**, set your hours and appointment length, and save. Open the schedule, choose **Open booking page** (or **Share**), and copy the link. It looks like `https://calendar.app.google/…`. Paste it here with the provider set to **Google Calendar appointment page**. Bookings land straight in that Google Calendar.
  - **Calendly:** paste your event link (`https://calendly.com/…`).
  - Switching accounts later (for example to your own Gmail) is just pasting the new link here; nothing else changes.
  - If the link doesn't match the provider you picked, the page tells you which one to choose.
- **Services**: the cuts you offer, each with an optional one-line description. They appear in the **Services** section of your home page and as the "Service" choices on the request form. Drag to reorder, and turn one off to hide it everywhere. (No prices are ever shown.)
- **Opening Hours**: type your hours for each day, like "9am–6pm" or "Closed", plus an optional note ("By appointment only"). Days with the same hours are grouped on your site ("Tue–Thu 9am–6pm"). Leave a day empty to leave it out.
- **Contact**: phone, public email, directions link.
- **Social Links**: Instagram and Facebook. Icons appear in the footer only for the ones you fill in.
- **SEO**: how your site looks in Google and when a link is shared, plus a sharing picture. Only write things that are true.

Every field tells you what's expected. If something isn't allowed (for example a link that doesn't start with **https://**), you'll see a plain message right by the field.

## "Unsaved changes"

Edit pages show **All changes saved**, **Unsaved changes** or **Saved ✓**. If you try to leave with unsaved changes you'll be asked first, so you can't lose work by accident.

On a computer you can press **Ctrl+S** (**⌘+S** on a Mac) to save, just like in other programs. Most settings pages also have a **See it on your website ↗** button that opens the part of your site you're editing in a new tab.

## Handy extras

- **Announcement status:** *Website Content → Announcement* tells you in plain words whether your notice is **showing now**, **scheduled** to start, or has **ended**, and it updates as you change the dates. The Dashboard shows the same status whenever an announcement is set.
- **Google preview:** *Settings → SEO* shows roughly how your site will look in Google search results as you type, and warns you if the title or description is long enough to be cut off.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You appear to be offline" | Check your signal or Wi-Fi and tap the button again. |
| A photo is rejected | Read the message. It says why (wrong format, too small, too large). Fix that and choose it again. |
| A video won't upload | Tap **Save** again: it picks up where it stopped. If it says the file is too big, trim it shorter or compress it. |
| "You don't have permission" or "session expired" | Sign out and sign back in. |
| Saved, but the website looks the same | Refresh the page on your website. If it still hasn't changed, check the item is **Published** (not a Draft). |
| Anything else | Take a screenshot and send it to your developer. |

## Client privacy

Please don't upload: photos where a client's face is clearly shown without their OK; anything showing a client's name, phone number or appointment details; screenshots of your booking calendar; or ID cards, receipts or payment details. When in doubt, leave it out or ask the client first.
