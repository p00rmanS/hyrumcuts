Privacy

Privacy notice

This page explains what information is collected through the HyrumHaircuts website and how it's used. It covers the appointment-request form and is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

What is collected

When you send an appointment request we collect your name, phone number, email address, requested date and time window, whether you're a first-time or returning client, any optional notes you add, and the fact that you agreed to be contacted. The website's hosting provider may also log technical details, such as an IP address, to prevent spam and abuse.

Why it's collected

This information is used only to schedule, confirm and manage your appointment, and to contact you about it by text or email. It is not sold, and it is not used for marketing you haven't asked for.

How requests are handled

Requests are received through the site's hosting provider (Netlify Forms) and passed to Hyrum. The website's photos, videos and text are stored with a separate content service (Supabase), which does not receive your request details.

Live calendar scheduling

If you book through the live calendar instead of the request form, that scheduling happens directly with the calendar provider (for example Calendly), under its own privacy policy, and not through this website.

How long it's kept

Appointment requests are kept for as long as they're needed to manage your appointments. You can ask for your information to be deleted at any time using the contact method below, and it will be removed unless there's a legitimate reason to keep it.

Your request, deletion, or questions

To view, correct or delete the information tied to a request, use the contact details on this website and include your name and the date of your request.

Analytics

No analytics or tracking scripts are loaded. If an analytics tool is ever added, this page will be updated to name it.
